## Stratagem #2 – Fine Tune Metabolism

Your metabolism is the chemical process inside your body that is necessary in order to maintain life. This can be further subdivided into constructive metabolism,which is the synthesis of proteins carbohydrates and fats to form tissue and store energy, and destructive metabolism, which is the breakdown of complex substances, and the consequent production of energy and waste matter.

If you’ve spent hours on the treadmill or strength training, months depriving yourself of the food you love but something still feels off – like you’re not getting the weight loss or lean muscle build up you expected, Fine-tuning your metabolism  is likely the piece that’s missing. 

Without optimization of metabolism, you can continue on to the next weight loss trend or personal trainer routine and still not  feel and look your best.
