Springfield, Mo., Oct. 27, 2026 – A New You Women's Clinic is a 2026 Better Business Bureau (BBB) Springfield Torch Award for Ethics winner.

BBB Torch Awards for Ethics recognize the businesses and organizations in our communities that meet the highest standards of trust. Five businesses are being recognized in the Springfield region this year.

“These honorees represent the kind of people and businesses that make our local marketplace stronger,” said Pamela Hernandez, BBB Springfield Regional Director. “Each has demonstrated a genuine, deep commitment to doing the right thing and helping others. We’re proud to celebrate their efforts with these awards.”

A New You Women's Clinic provides compassionate, personalized and high-quality women’s health care in the Springfield region. The clinic focuses specifically on the issues that impact women the most, offering empowerment and solutions for those who have struggled to find a care provider who will listen.

The clinic’s work is centered on the belief that healthcare should be ethical, transparent and patient-centered. Staff receive ongoing education and training, and they ensure patients understand their options, expected outcomes, risks, and responsibilities before beginning any treatment. A New You Women’s Clinic also brings community service into their work by offering complimentary educational seminars, wellness resources, participation in local health fairs and other outreach efforts related to women’s health, hormone optimization, weight loss, anti-aging, inflammation, metabolism, and preventative wellness. They provide in-kind services wherever possible and support the Breast Cancer Foundation of the Ozarks with fundraising efforts.

“Winning the 2026 Torch Award for Ethics is such an honor. It means a lot to us because we work hard every day to provide honest, ethical, patient-centered care. At A New You Women’s Clinic, we believe every woman deserves to be heard, informed, and cared for as a whole person. This award encourages us to keep doing what we do and earning the trust of the women we serve,” said Cindy Sparks, Founder.

## About BBB

BBB is a nonprofit, business-supported organization that sets and upholds high standards for fair and honest business behavior. BBB services to consumers are free. BBB provides objective advice, BBB Business Profiles on more than 5.3 million companies, charity reviews, dispute resolution services, alerts and educational information on topics affecting marketplace trust. Visit [BBB.org](https://www.bbb.org/) for more information.
