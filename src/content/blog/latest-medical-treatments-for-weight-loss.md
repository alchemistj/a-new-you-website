Losing weight isn’t just about willpower—your biology plays a huge role. That’s why at *A New You Women’s Clinic*, our [weight loss treatments](/springfield/weight-loss/) offer cutting-edge, FDA-approved medications that help treat obesity as a medical condition, not a personal failure.

Here’s a closer look at today’s most effective medical weight loss treatments—and how they may be the key to [lasting results for men](/springfield/mens-health/) and women.

## Semaglutide (Wegovy®, Ozempic®, Rybelsus®)

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist—a class of drugs that mimics hormones that regulate appetite and blood sugar.

-   **Wegovy®** is FDA-approved for weight loss in adults with obesity or overweight with at least one weight-related condition.
-   **Ozempic®** is approved for type 2 diabetes but is often prescribed off-label for weight loss.
-   **Rybelsus®** is a tablet, FDA-approved for diabetes and prescribed off-label for weight loss.

Benefits:

-   Appetite suppression
-   Slows digestion for prolonged fullness
-   Avg. weight loss: ~15% of body weight
-   Weekly injection (Wegovy/Ozempic) or daily pill (Rybelsus)

Clinical Reference: [Wegovy Prescribing Info – FDA](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/215256s000lbl.pdf)

## Tirzepatide (Zepbound®, Mounjaro®)

Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist, and it’s the newest and most powerful option available.

-   **Mounjaro®** is FDA-approved to treat type 2 diabetes and is often prescribed off-label for weight loss due to its strong appetite-suppressing effects.
-   **Zepbound®** was FDA-approved in November 2023 specifically for chronic weight management in adults with obesity or overweight and a related health condition.

Benefits:

-   Superior hunger control
-   Supports insulin sensitivity
-   Avg. weight loss: up to 20% of body weight
-   Weekly injection

Clinical Reference: [Zepbound FDA Approval News](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-new-medication-chronic-weight-management)

## Liraglutide (Saxenda®)

Saxenda® is part of the GLP-1 family of medications and has been used for over a decade.

-   **FDA-approved** for chronic weight management in both adults and adolescents aged 12 and older.
-   Taken as a daily injection, Saxenda is a trusted option for those looking for a consistent, well-studied medication.

Benefits:

-   Helps regulate appetite and blood sugar
-   Suitable for adults and teens (12–17)
-   Avg. weight loss: 5–8% of body weight

Clinical Reference: [Saxenda Prescribing Info – FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/fda-approves-weight-management-drug-patients-aged-12-and-older)

## Phentermine

Phentermine is a prescription appetite suppressant that’s been used safely in clinical practice since the 1950s. It works by stimulating the central nervous system, similar to how amphetamines do, to help reduce hunger and increase energy levels.

-   It’s **FDA-approved for short-term use**, usually 12 weeks or less.
-   Often used as a jumpstart option, particularly for patients needing initial motivation or quick early results.

Benefits:

-   Suppresses appetite
-   Boosts energy and metabolism
-   Usually prescribed for 12 weeks or less
-   Avg. weight loss: 3–5% body weight

**Note:** Not suitable for patients with heart conditions or high blood pressure.

Clinical Reference: [FDA Info on Phentermine](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/fda-approves-treatment-chronic-weight-management-pediatric-patients-aged-12-years-and-older)

## Naltrexone/Bupropion (Contrave®)

**Contrave®** combines two medications—naltrexone (used for addiction treatment) and bupropion (used for depression and smoking cessation)—to target brain regions controlling hunger and reward.

Benefits:

-   Reduces cravings and emotional eating
-   Oral tablet taken twice daily
-   Avg. weight loss: 4–9%

Clinical Reference: [Contrave Prescribing Info – FDA](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/200063s000lbl.pdf)
